Member invitation email

Sends an email containing a signup link. The recipient must complete email verification after registering before they can log in normally. The role (permissions) is fixed to the value selected here.

Recipient flow

  1. Open the invitation email. Remind them to check spam and promotions folders too.
  2. Click the «Sign up» link in the email to go to the registration page.
  3. Email and role are pre-filled from the invitation; they only enter username, name, password, etc.
  4. After signup, verify email using the link in the verification email.

You cannot invite someone who already registered with the same email. In that case, direct them to log in or use password recovery.

Security: the current admin API is unprotected for demo use. In production, add admin authentication and audit logging.

Operator checklist

  • Double-check the recipient email address (a typo may grant access to the wrong person).
  • Choose the minimum role that matches their actual duties.
  • In the optional message, briefly note company name, contact person, and next steps so the recipient understands context.
  • When sending externally, confirm the signup link in the send result starts with https://….
